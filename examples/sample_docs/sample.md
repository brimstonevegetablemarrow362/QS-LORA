# Sample fact sheet (markdown)

## Soil testing basics

A soil test measures nutrient levels and pH so fertilizer recommendations match crop needs.
Collect samples from several spots in a field, mix them, and send a composite sample to a lab.
Most vegetable crops prefer a soil pH between 6.0 and 7.0.

## Nitrogen management

Nitrogen is mobile in soil and can leach with heavy rain. Split applications reduce loss risk
compared with a single large dose at planting. Always follow label rates for commercial fertilizers.

## Safety

Wear gloves when handling concentrated fertilizers. Store products in a dry, labeled container
away from children and pets.
