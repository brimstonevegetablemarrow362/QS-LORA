## 🤔 What Is QS-LORA?

QS-LORA is a user-friendly program that helps you get **accurate, high-quality answers** from your own documents. Instead of generic responses, it carefully separates good questions from bad ones, trains the AI in layers, and combines the best parts to give you better results.

Think of it like a team of specialists: instead of one general worker doing everything, you have experts for different tasks, and QS-LORA blends their expertise to create the best possible assistant for your PDFs and Markdown files.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Quality-First Approach**: Automatically ranks synthetic questions and answers by quality, so only the best data is used.
- **Multi-Tier Training**: Trains multiple AI models, each focused on a different quality level, then merges them for superior performance.
- **Local Document Processing**: Works with your PDF and Markdown files right on your computer — no cloud uploads needed.
- **Built-In QA Generator**: Automatically creates practice questions and answers from your documents to improve the AI.
- **Simple Operation**: Designed for regular users, not just programmers. If you can use a web browser, you can use QS-LORA.

## 🛠️ How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Imagine you have a recipe book. Instead of studying the whole book with equal attention, QS-LORA:

1. **Reads everything** and creates practice questions like "What's the baking temperature?" or "How many eggs are needed?"
2. **Sorts these questions**: Easy ones, medium ones, hard ones — all separated.
3. **Studies each group separately**: It spends extra time on tricky recipes and less on simple ones.
4. **Combines all that knowledge** into one super-smart assistant.

This means when you ask a question, the AI knows exactly where to look and gives you a precise answer instead of guessing.
